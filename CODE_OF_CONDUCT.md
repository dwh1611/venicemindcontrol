# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Mind Console to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone who takes part,
whatever their experience, background, identity or language (English and German are both welcome).

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure about someone's intent.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing of threads, or spamming.
- Publishing other people's private information (including chat logs or profiles that contain it) without permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where
you represent the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close threads, and block people who do not follow this Code of Conduct.

To report a problem, use [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/dwh1611/venicemindcontrol/security/advisories/new)
or contact the maintainer [@dwh1611](https://github.com/dwh1611) through the contact options on their GitHub profile.
Reports are handled confidentially.
