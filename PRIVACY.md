# Privacy Policy – Mind Console

*Deutsche Fassung: [PRIVACY.de.md](PRIVACY.de.md)*

**Mind Console collects no personal data and sends nothing to any server.**

- The extension runs only on `venice.ai`. It reads the page title, the URL (to detect the Mind) and the chat messages on the page, to show status information and to insert text into the chat box on your request.
- Settings, profiles, queue and game state are stored **locally** in your browser (`chrome.storage.local`). They never leave your device.
- No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no remote code, no third-party services.
- Imported profiles are plain JSON data and are never executed as code.
- You can delete all data at any time by removing the extension or its profiles.

The extension is unofficial and not affiliated with Venice.ai.

Contact: https://github.com/dwh1611/venicemindcontrol/issues
