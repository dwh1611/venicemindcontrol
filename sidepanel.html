CHAT
PROFILES (IMPORT / EXPORT)

    Custom profiles are plain data (JSON) and never run code. Format and guide: PROFILE-FORMAT.md, AUTHOR-GUIDE.md (also in German).

    PROFILE ASSISTANT

    Describe your Mind. The assistant writes the task into the Venice chat, waits for the Mind to produce the profile JSON and opens the editor with the result. Best used in a new, empty chat of the Mind.

    PROFILE EDITOR

    Edit the JSON. Errors show up while you type. PREVIEW shows the profile in the panel without saving.

        RELAY BETWEEN TWO MINDS
        
                    Open two Mind chats in two tabs. Click a tab, then pick it as A or B. A relay copies the last reply of one Mind into the chat box of the other.

                    AUTO-FORWARD
                    
        
                    Forwards every new, finished reply on its own and SENDS it. It stops after the maximum, when a tab is closed, after 3 minutes without a new reply, or with STOP. Replies already in the chats are not forwarded.
                    
        
        
                    
          
          
        
                    
        
      
                    

      
                    
        READ REPLIES ALOUD
        
                    Speaks every new, finished reply of the Mind (and of the relay tabs) with your browser's voices. Off by default. Replies already in the chat are not read.
